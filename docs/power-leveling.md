# Power Leveling in WoW Forever

## What the player sees

Power Leveling starts automatically on login. Existing profiles using the old Batch default switch once. Players can choose a different style in Settings. The compact panel gives one instruction. Near a level, its header shows the target level and estimated time. A hand-in shows the immediate reward and projected XP left. Hover for trip totals and the estimated time for ready hand-ins alone when a different route saves time. Expand the panel with **+** to see the trip grouped by stop, with NPCs, coordinates, transport and each reward. The same itinerary is available to copy with `/qt route`. `/qt route bands` lists catalog zones for your race and faction. `/qt route 21` previews up to three trips in the 21–25 band, starting at the character's current position with their race, faction and completed prerequisites.

The Barrens example shows why the choice matters. With 3,663 XP to level 22, Tribes at War (1,650 XP) and Kill Grundig Darkcloud (1,350 XP) leave 663 XP. Mura Runetotem gives this character another 850 XP in Silverpine Forest. There is also a conditional Barrens route. Handing in Tribes unlocks Blood Shards of Agamaggan with Mangletooth in Camp Taurajo. That follow-up offers 1,250 base XP and needs one Blood Shard. With the shard in the player's bags, the three Barrens rewards total 4,250 XP. Without it the route includes harvesting time. If other eligible work reaches 22 sooner, it can win. The actual first stop depends on position, quest progress, transport and available offers.

## How trips are chosen

* Throughout the level the planner estimates quest reward XP divided by the time for pickup, objectives, travel and hand-in. It tries combinations of nearby quests and confirmed prerequisite relationships. A completed quest starts at its hand-in step. A quest in progress starts with its remaining work.
* In the last quarter of a level, or whenever known ready rewards cover the XP gap, the planner searches for trips that reach the next level. It tries ready-only routes, mixed routes, eligible pickups and immediate follow-ups. Each continuation starts at the previous hand-in and inherits its transport cooldown usage and remaining fare budget. It compares elapsed time up to the level and discards unnecessary stops. The search bounds candidate count so it can run during play. It provides an estimate among the routes tested.
* Public transport, eligible portals and confirmed flights can be linked with walking, including two rides in one leg. A flight path can be a suggested discovery before either end has been learned. A flight recommendation requires a reachable connection reported by the flight master and enough money. A measured landing replaces the route's distance-based duration. Hearth, Astral Recall and personal teleports depend on bind location, inventory, learned spells and cooldowns as applicable.
* Players can opt in with `/qt party on`. In a normal party the leader's current eligible quest can align the group's steps. A nearby member who reports missing credits can keep the group on the objective. Shared travel requires recently confirmed affordable departures from participating members. Personal teleports and hearths are left out of group travel. Party messages pause when combat or the client's chat restrictions prevent sharing.
* The existing timer preference can promote an expiring quest. The player still chooses whether to move, accept, turn in, board or cast. `/qt stats` shows session and recent XP/hour, time to level when there is enough history, and objective credit pace when observed. A slow credit rate includes competition, drop luck, combat and travel within the camp. It cannot identify the server's respawn timer for a particular mob.

Counts cover both factions combined. The generator selects quests in the chosen hub regions with a known giver, finisher, objective locations and an observed base reward. A level band describes quest levels in the catalog. Following that band's quests alone may leave an XP gap for a particular race, character or starting position. New characters, skipped chains and characters who leveled in dungeons can enter through the same planner. The in-game offer and completion state determine which route is plausible.

Regenerate the quest snapshot with `node tools/build-route-catalog.cjs <QuestieDB-directory>` using the Forever 1.1.3 distribution. The generator reads the baked TOC, the Forever map conversion and the observed XP support table. Static names are English. The game supplies localized held-quest names. The selected data includes entity names and factual fields. Instructions and route ordering are authored in QuestTriage. ## Where to compete and how to measure it

RestedXP offers Forever leveling guides. Zygor offers Forever guides and documents both travel directions and group guide sharing. A list of flight paths, group messaging or an arrow alone gives players little reason to switch. QuestTriage's opportunity is the mid-level decision a returning player actually faces. It can explain why two local hand-ins and a follow-up are a better use of the next few minutes, and change the answer for a partner who still needs an objective. [RestedXP Forever](https://shop.restedxp.com/forever/) · [Zygor Forever](https://zygorguides.com/forever) · [Zygor Travel System](https://content.zygorguides.com/support/manual/travel) · [Zygor Share Mode](https://forum.zygorguides.com/support/manual/sharemode)

1. **Measure walking first.** Straight-line yardage can make a cliff, mountain pass or awkward dock appear cheap. Compare the current estimates with Shortest Path Forever walking times and actual walks through the Barrens and Stonetalon. Record the largest errors before expanding the transport graph.
2. **Calibrate quest and camp times.** Compare predicted objective time with elapsed time from the first credit to the last. Separate kills, item drops, named spawns and existing items in the bags. Track sample counts per camp and group size. The current planner uses measured progress when available and falls back to estimates.
3. **Time the same leveling decisions.** Start equivalent characters at the same XP, location, quest state, funds, bind, learned paths and rested bonus. Run a fresh starter band and the level-21 Barrens case solo and as a duo. Repeat each run on each guide, alternate their order, and report median and range for minutes to level, travel minutes, deaths and redundant quest-giver visits. In a duo, stop the clock when both players reach the target.

The automated tests cover the Barrens trade-off, follow-up inventory, quest eligibility, transport direction and group flight confirmation with simulated client state. Actual terrain, live server offers, crowded camps, rendering and comparative leveling speed still require in-game runs.
