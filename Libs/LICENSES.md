## Power Leveling data

QuestTriage selects quest IDs, entity names, coordinates, numeric rewards and prerequisite relationships from QuestieDB Forever 1.1.3 (8b8df2d). Credit to Questie, QuestieDB and the players contributing Forever observations. The source documents ownership per file and historic contributions. QuestTriage's route ordering and player instructions are original. [QuestieDB provenance](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/PROVENANCE.md) · [QuestieDB license](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/LICENSE.md)
